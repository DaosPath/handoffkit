# HandoffKit 1.60-1.69 — Distributed Platform

> Status: **planned**. A multi-node schema is not a distributed runtime.

## Goal

Evolve qualified local apps and workers into a federated, durable platform with explicit trust, placement, replication, observability, and recovery semantics.

## Distributed invariants

- Identity remains bound to verified credentials, never peer-supplied JSON.
- Authorization is evaluated locally at every trust boundary.
- Cryptographic replay protection is distinct from business deduplication.
- Delivery, consistency, retry, and ordering guarantees are documented per operation.
- Data placement and replication respect policy and trust domain.
- Capability advertisements are measured from active providers.
- A partition must fail according to an explicit consistency policy, not silent fallback.

## Required failure testing

- process, node, and network loss;
- delayed, duplicated, reordered, and corrupted messages;
- expired and rotated credentials;
- partial upgrades and incompatible nodes;
- disk-full and corrupted durable state;
- split brain and clock skew;
- artifact signer revocation;
- restore from independently verified backups.

## Non-goals for this train

- claiming multi-region availability from deployment manifests alone;
- hiding consistency tradeoffs behind automatic retries;
- accepting peer-asserted tenant or capability identity;
- supporting an environment that cannot pass the conformance and recovery suite.
